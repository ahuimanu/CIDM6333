CIDM 6333 · Agentic Systems Design and Development

Lectures

One lecture deck for each Evolution, with the orientation deck and two mini lectures beside them. Each page shows the slides with the lecture written out beneath them.

Orientation · 33 slidesCourse OrientationHow the course runs, in the order a student meets each part of it. The Evolutions and the Sprint and Drift rhythm come first, then flight cards and sorties, the Chase, the two toolkit routes, and the Final System.E0 · Index Zero · 21 slidesThe Human Is Index ZeroStarts from the layers of AI and the progression from AI to agentic AI, asks what makes a system an agent, and ends with the Degradation Pattern, the covenant, and the five gates that keep the human at Index zero.E1 · The Agent Loop · 13 slidesThe Agent LoopBuilds an agent out of a single model call, by way of chain-of-thought and ReAct, and then sets the loop that has no halt beside the loop that writes a ledger.E2 · Patterns and Frameworks · 13 slidesPatterns and Orchestration FrameworksSeparates workflows from agents, lays out the five patterns, argues that every framework is a theory of who owns the loop, and ends with the build: the loop refactored into one of the patterns, with the choice recorded in ADR-1.E3 · Tools as Contracts · 11 slidesTools as ContractsTools as contracts with a schema and a side-effect class, and the permission gating, retries, and halting the harness puts around them. The model is an untrusted caller, and hooks run between its call and the tool.E4 · MCP I · 12 slidesMCP I: Building a ServerWhy MCP turns N×M integrations into N+M, then hosts, clients, and servers, the three primitives (tools, resources, prompts), and the stateless 2026 revision. The build is one server that two clients call, with every call logged.E5 · MCP II, Security, Harness · 23 slidesMCP II: Security and the HarnessTransports, authorization, and scope first, then the threat model, A2A set against MCP, and instruction files and skills. The deck ends on harness engineering, under the framing sentence that the product is the harness.E6 · Context Engineering · 23 slidesContext EngineeringTreats the context window as an artifact with a budget, since on every turn the model knows what is in the window and nothing else. The deck then covers compaction, progressive disclosure, subagents, handoffs, and the layers of truth.E7 · Memory · 25 slidesMemory: Retrieval, State, Eviction, RetentionDefines memory as the part of context that survives the session, then asks four questions of any store. What is kept, who wrote it, how is it found again, and when is it deleted.E8 · Multi-Agent I · 23 slidesMulti-agent I: Controller, Worker, ReviewerPlan, do, check has gone under several names since FIPA, and the deck traces them before it builds the controller, worker, and reviewer out of roles and personas, result contracts that fail closed, two-stage review, and a state graph with a human interrupt.E9 · Multi-Agent II · 23 slidesMulti-agent II: When Coordination FailsOpens with the taxonomy of how multi-agent systems fail, then answers it with adversarial validation in three tiers, locks on shared work, cost-aware routing, delegation and handoff, and the contracts an agent marketplace would need.E10 · Evals as Gates · 23 slidesEvaluation I: Evals as GatesGolden sets, regression deltas, judge biases, the RED witness, and pre-registration make an eval into a gate. The second half reads five agent benchmarks for what each one measures and what it hides.E11 · Observability and Red Team · 22 slidesEvaluation II: Receipts, Red Team, SafetyTraces and receipts are the black box of an agent run, and cost and latency come out of it. The nine failure modes become red-team probes, system cards count as evaluation documents, and guardrails belong at an enforcement locus.E12 · Deployment · 23 slidesProduction DeploymentTakes a system from working to operated, through serving, containers, secrets, limits, scheduling, and rollback, and ends with the Heavy-lane gates and the release ceremony that a release must pass.Mini lecture · Attention (2017) · 11 slidesAttention Is All You Need, read as a design documentReads the 2017 paper as a design document, in which the authors state a constraint, make one trade against it, and back the trade with evidence. Every model your harness calls descends from this paper.Mini lecture · Steer the Middle · 14 slidesSteer the Middle: three rings, one bar, E0 to E8Prepares the follow-along exercise. The model, OpenCode, and your harness are drawn as three rings, a table separates what OpenCode already has from what you write, and the last slides build the reviewer that cannot edit.

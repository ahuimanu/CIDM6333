Two Loops

A language model is a loop inside a loop. The inner loop is the network writing one token per pass. The outer loop is the harness you build: it assembles the context, reads the reply, runs the tool, and decides when to stop. Step through the figure with Next, the dots, or the arrow keys.

The draw at the end of every pass

The model scores every token in its vocabulary. Softmax turns scores into probabilities and the sampler draws one. The scores here are made up for teaching; a real vocabulary has on the order of a hundred thousand entries.

What the figure leaves out

  • Normalization layers inside each block. They keep the numbers in range; they do not change the flow.
  • Mixture-of-experts models replace the one MLP with several expert MLPs and a router that picks a few per token. The loop is the same.
  • The figure in Vaswani et al. (2017) shows an encoder and a decoder (see Figure 01b). Chat models of the GPT, Claude, and Llama lineage use the decoder side only, which is what this figure draws.
  • Hosted APIs return tool calls as structured blocks, not raw text. The model still produced them token by token; the provider's harness parsed them before yours sees them.

Reference design · gzkit ADR-0.0.2 · E2 slide 10

Popularity is not rationale

Decision
Build gzkit's CLI on the standard library's argparse rather than click.
Alternatives
click, typer, argparse, each with consequences, not adjectives.
Rejected reason
Click's popularity.
Reasons that counted
Zero external dependencies for a governance kit; a smaller attack and upgrade surface; the stdlib-first posture that later became doctrine.

The deck summarizes this ADR from its title and the book's outline, and flags its alternatives and consequences for checking against the ADR text. The stakes are low and the reasoning is clean, which is why the deck uses it as the model.

The shape of ADR-1
  1. Intent. What the loop must do, from the PRD.
  2. Decision. Pattern plus framework, in one sentence.
  3. Alternatives. At least three, including none / stdlib, each with what it buys and what it costs.
  4. Consequences. Positive and negative, and the one you will measure in Sortie 2.
  5. Decomposition scorecard. The decision scored against the alternatives on the criteria you named.
  6. Feature checklist. What the choice must support by E12: tools, MCP, memory, multi-agent, evals, serving.
ADRs before code. After is post-hoc rationalization.E2 deck, slide 10

Before you record the decision, run the reference design's three forcing functions. A pre-mortem writes the story of the choice failing in week twelve. WWHTBT asks what would have to be true for the rejected alternative to have been right. Constraint archaeology traces each constraint to the PRD, the syllabus, or habit, and habit does not count.

What the figure leaves out

  • Patterns combine in real systems. A router can send one class into a chain and another into an agent. The figure draws each pattern alone.
  • Ownership here is coarse. "You" covers very different work: drawing a graph edge, writing a Pydantic type, writing a hook, or pressing allow on a permission prompt.
  • The figure carries no cost or quality numbers. Sortie 2 produces them, from your own two runs.
  • Week eight returns to the role-based frameworks with FIPA and BDI, and to orchestrator–workers with subagents. Week ten returns to evaluator–optimizer as evaluation as a gate.
  • The deck's ADR-0.0.2 summary and its vendor SDK table both carry verify-before-class flags. The figure repeats them as the deck states them.

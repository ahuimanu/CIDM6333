Steer the Middle

Three rings and one bar. The inner ring is the model. The middle ring is OpenCode, the loop you did not write. The outer ring is what you write to steer the middle: one file for each thing OpenCode already does by default, plus two additions. The bar says where the human's work is: wide before the loop runs and wide after, narrow where the model runs. Step from the inside out, then from E0 to E8.

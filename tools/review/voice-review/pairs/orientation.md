### BEFORE

A flight card is a one-page form you fill in before you touch the terminal. It comes from gzkit’s flight-test program, which borrowed it from aircraft flight test: nobody just flies it and sees. Envelope position says how far from the known-good center this sortie goes. The design claim is one sentence that a single observation could disprove.

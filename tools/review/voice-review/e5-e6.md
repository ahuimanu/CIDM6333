### 1. E5 slide 9 (lecture), category 1, CHANGED
- BEFORE: "The strip is the trap: a signature answers who, never whether."
- AFTER: "a signed Agent Card proves who sent the artifact, and whether the artifact is true remains a check your host has to make."
- Problem: A2A v1.0 signs the Agent Card (the agent's identity and capabilities), not each artifact, so the AFTER sentence claims the card signature authenticates the artifact's sender, which BEFORE never said.
- Fix: "A signed Agent Card tells you which agent you are talking to; it says nothing about whether that agent's artifact is true, and your host has to check that."

### 2. E5 slide 1 (lecture), category 1, CHANGED + ADDED causal claim
- BEFORE: "the product is the harness, and most of what makes an agent product safe or unsafe is not the model."
- AFTER: "The reason is that the model is one part of an agent product and the one part you cannot change, so whatever makes the product safe or unsafe sits in the parts you can."
- Problem: "most" became "whatever" (all), and the added premise "the one part you cannot change" is false in this course, where students pick a model (Big Pickle, a Go subscription, a "second model family" in E6 slide 23).
- Fix: Restore the scope qualifier and drop the invented premise: "The framing sentence is that the product is the harness: most of what makes an agent product safe or unsafe sits outside the model, in parts you can test."

### 3. E5 slide 3 (lecture), category 1, ADDED causal claim
